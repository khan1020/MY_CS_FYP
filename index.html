About The Project

This AI chatbot is a Final Year Project developed to demonstrate the capabilities of modern artificial intelligence in natural language processing and conversation.

The system is designed to understand context, provide relevant responses, and learn from interactions to improve over time.

Our goal is to create an AI assistant that can be integrated into various platforms to enhance user experience and provide valuable assistance.
